From cherry to green bean

Processing shapes the sweetness.

The same coffee variety can taste remarkably different depending on how the fruit is removed and the seed is dried.

01

Washed

Clean & bright

The fruit is removed before drying, revealing crisp acidity and a clear expression of origin.

02

Natural

Sweet & full

The whole cherry dries around the seed, often creating ripe fruit, wine, and deeper sweetness.

03

Honey

Round & balanced

Some sticky fruit mucilage remains during drying, bridging washed clarity and natural sweetness.
